## A Mediterranean take on fashion

Our [Nice boutique](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/toutes-nos-boutiques-mode/lulli-nice) belongs to a sunny, easy-going way of life. It sits on rue de la Liberté, between place Masséna and the sea, where people like to stroll before heading down to the Promenade.

Across 200 m², everything was designed to recall the coast. A poured beige floor, bright arches, limewashed walls and furniture whose textures borrow from citrus fruit. The result is a warm, light-filled cocoon where the selection reveals itself at an unhurried pace. Many come in for one specific piece and leave with the idea of a whole outfit.

## Other concept stores to discover in Nice

Nice is full of characterful addresses, and we would be the last to claim we are the only one. **ICI Concept Store**, rue Tonduti de l'Escarène, specialises in niche perfumes, beauty and homeware with an eco-conscious spirit. **Trésors Publics**, rue de la Préfecture, gathers iconic everyday objects made in France. And **Hattori**, rue Catherine Segurane, champions womenswear from independent designers.

Each has its own colour. With us, the wardrobe leads the way, designer pieces, bags and shoes, backed by a network of boutiques across the south-east. Moving from one to the next is often the best way to find the address that feels like you.

## Frequently asked questions

At 5 rue de la Liberté, right in the centre of Nice, a short walk from place Masséna and a few minutes from the sea. The boutique is open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm, and you can reach us on +33 4 86 57 40 25.

**Can I collect an online order in Nice?**
Yes, Click and Collect is available for the rue de la Liberté boutique. You order on our website, then come in to try on and pick up your piece during opening hours.
