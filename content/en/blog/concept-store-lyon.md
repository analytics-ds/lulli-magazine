A question has come up often lately, what is the best concept store in Lyon? The answer depends on what you are looking for, and we do not claim to be the only one who can answer it. But we do have an address in the city, and it felt more honest to introduce it directly than to let you discover it by chance.

## A boutique in the 2nd arrondissement

Our [Lyon boutique](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/toutes-nos-boutiques-mode/boutique-mode-lyon) sits at 5 rue des Quatre Chapeaux, in the 2nd arrondissement. It is open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm.

The space plays on clean volumes, poured concrete and warm beige tones, with bespoke furniture in clean lines. It is a historic boutique, where the architecture stays quiet so the pieces on display can speak for themselves.

## What you will find there

The selection on show in Lyon blends signatures found across our whole network with designers specifically highlighted in this boutique: Ami Paris, Anine Bing, Arizona Love, Asics, Barbour, Birkenstock, Claris Virot, Clarks, Dragon Diffusion, Essentiel Antwerp, Jérôme Dreyfuss, Margaux Lonnberg, [Ginette NY](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/createurs/ginette-ny.html) and Toral.

You will find clothing, shoes, bags and jewellery there, following the same logic as in our other addresses, we buy our own [selection](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/createurs.html), we do not connect third-party sellers.

## The concept store, our model since 2003

A concept store stands apart from a department store through what it chooses not to sell. We have held this model since our first boutique opened in Marseille in 2003, under the impetus of Anne Vouland. The selection has grown over the years, to around twenty addresses across the south-east of France, while keeping the same principle, a team that chooses, season after season, rather than a referencing catalogue.

## Our favourite addresses near the boutique

The neighbourhood is worth exploring beyond the boutique itself. Here are the addresses we recommend to our customers visiting Lyon:

- **Coco Lyon**, for a chic break and timeless Lyonnaise elegance.
- **Bartholomé Brunch**, for a brunch or a sweet pause.
- **Les Infidèles**, raw and charming, for a moment out of time.
- **Carmelo**, for Italian cooking in a warm setting with generous plates.
- **Le Mercière**, a Lyonnaise bouchon where people come for the convivial atmosphere and the classics that never disappoint.

## Services, in practice

Click and Collect is available, order online and collect your piece in store. Delivery is free from 150 euros of purchase, returns are free within 14 days, and payment can be split into three or four instalments. Our customers rate us 4.83 out of 5 in verified reviews.

## Other addresses worth knowing in Lyon

We are not the only boutique of this kind in the city, and it feels more honest to say so. **Hyppairs**, place Sathonay in the 1st arrondissement, and **Little Liamone**, quai de la Pêcherie, each carry a selection different from ours. The three addresses do not share the same positioning, and comparing them is often the best way to find the boutique that suits you.

## Frequently asked questions

**What exactly is a concept store?**
It is a boutique that does not champion a single label but a curated selection, chosen and stood behind by a team that buys its own stock. Unlike a department store, the offer stays tight and coherent across categories, from clothing to jewellery. This is the model we have held since 2003.

**Where is the Lulli boutique in Lyon?**
At 5 rue des Quatre Chapeaux, in the 2nd arrondissement. It is open Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm, and you can reach us on +33 4 88 00 89 89.

**What other concept stores are worth knowing in Lyon?**
Lyon has several fine addresses in this format, notably Hyppairs, place Sathonay, and Little Liamone, quai de la Pêcherie, both in the 1st arrondissement. Each carries a different selection from ours, and that is exactly the point of comparing before you choose.

**Can I use Click and Collect at the Lyon boutique?**
Yes. You can order online and collect your piece in store, during opening hours Monday to Saturday.

**What gift can I find at the Lyon boutique?**
The boutique displays a tight selection of jewellery and accessories, faster to browse than a full department, with pieces from designers such as Ginette NY or Dragon Diffusion. For a full wardrobe, the clothing and shoe collections are also on display.
