## An address between lake and mountains

Our [Annecy boutique](https://www.lulli-sur-la-toile.com/toutes-nos-boutiques-mode/annecy) sits on rue de la Poste, in the gentle rhythm of the town centre, where people take their time before heading down to the lake. We designed it as a pause, a bright space with sober lines, where discreet materials and soft tones leave all the room to the pieces.

Light oak furniture frames the bags, baskets and shoes, and you move around naturally, without endless aisles. People often come in for one piece and stay for the atmosphere.

A visit to Annecy does not end on rue de la Poste. Between two fittings, here are our favourite places in the neighbourhood:

## Other addresses to know in Annecy

The centre of Annecy has other concept stores with character, and it would be a shame to leave them out. **babeth**, rue Royale, is a lifestyle house that combines fashion, furniture, decoration and a coffee shop. **Les Palettes**, rue de la Paix, champions emerging designers in jewellery, leather goods and design. And **Biutiful**, our neighbour at 4 bis rue de la Poste, focuses on beauty, fragrance and decoration.

These addresses do not share our positioning. With us, fashion leads, the designer wardrobe, shoes and bags. It is often by going from one to the other that you find the boutique that suits you.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where is the Lulli boutique in Annecy?**
At 6 rue de la Poste, in the centre of Annecy, a few minutes' walk from the lake. It is open Monday to Saturday from 10am to 7pm, and you can reach us on +33 4 50 63 32 96.

**Which designers can you find at the Annecy boutique?**
The boutique features Anine Bing, adidas, Asics, Dragon Diffusion, Forte_Forte, Fugazzi, Golden Goose, Ibeliv, Isabel Marant, New Balance, Sebago and Assouline books. You will find clothing, shoes, bags, accessories and a few pieces for the home.

**Can I collect an order at the Annecy boutique?**
Yes. You can order on our website and choose collection at the rue de la Poste boutique, during opening hours.

**Which other concept stores are worth knowing in Annecy?**
Annecy has several fine addresses in this format, each with a very different approach from ours. babeth, rue Royale, combines fashion, furniture and a café. Les Palettes, rue de la Paix, champions emerging designers in jewellery, leather goods and design. Biutiful, our neighbour on rue de la Poste, focuses on beauty, fragrance and decoration.

**Is the Annecy boutique open on Sundays?**
No. It is open Monday to Saturday, from 10am to 7pm. On Sundays our website remains available, with free delivery from 150 euros.
