Secret Santa comes with one simple rule and one real difficulty. The rule is the budget, set by the group. The difficulty is giving something beautiful to someone you sometimes barely know, without falling for the gadget that ends up in a drawer on 26 December.

So here are our Secret Santa gift ideas, organised by budget, because that is always the first question. Every piece mentioned is in stock, and every price was checked product by product on our website on 28 September 2026.

If you only remember one thing, the safest Secret Santa gift is the one that needs neither a size nor a precise taste. A home fragrance, a beauty treat, a mug. The riskiest is clothing, which is hard to choose for someone you do not know well.

## Three rules for a successful Secret Santa

**Respect the budget, both ways.** Going over the cap makes the recipient uncomfortable, and so does staying far below it. A 15 euro gift for a 15 euro cap is perfect.

**Give what people do not buy for themselves.** Nobody treats themselves to a room fragrance or a hairbrush cleaner. That is precisely why they please.

**Take care of the wrapping.** A small gift that is nicely wrapped always feels more generous. When you order, you can add gift wrapping, and the piece is sent already wrapped to the delivery address.
